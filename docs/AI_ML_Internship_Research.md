# AI/ML internship research and maintenance

Research date: **27 October 2026**. Scope: current India-based roles or remote roles explicitly available from India; undergraduate-compatible; no student fee. Paid, unpaid and undisclosed compensation are allowed. This is a maintenance ledger, not a student-facing list.

## Inclusion decision

The runtime catalog contains one cautiously mapped role: [LivingAI — Applied AI Engineer Intern](https://livingai.in/careers/#applied-ai-intern). The live employer accordion was opened and its role-specific email route checked without sending an application. The catalog preserves the stated stipend description; duration and deadline are unstated. No degree/year restriction is published. On-site availability remains an applicant decision, not an assumption made from semester.

Each mandatory subject maps to an existing course plus an actual selected assignment. The curriculum mapping covers Python, matrix/tensor foundations, ML fundamentals, language-model exploration and independent experimentation. The role's workplace responsibilities are learning activities, not additional stated entry qualifications. Curiosity and personal proficiency are not measured. There is no completion check, submission, grading or student test.

One role is intentional: research breadth does not justify padding the UI with partial matches. Different personalized roadmaps can legitimately have no results. Exercises involving tokenization, truncation or lexical retrieval are **not** evidence of fine-tuning, semantic/vector retrieval, deployment, industry experience or production engineering.

## Maintaining the catalog

1. Open the official role and application route without applying. Recheck current status, all mandatory requirements, preferred qualifications, geographic access, degree/year/institution rules, dates and fees. Do not infer availability from search snippets.
2. Edit `backend/src/main/resources/assessment/ai-ml-internships-v1.json`. Keep ISO UTC verification timestamps, and use an exact ISO instant for a deadline only when its timezone is established. If an actual deadline is ambiguous, withhold the role until resolved. Null means no published deadline; display other missing details as “Not stated”. Preserve compensation units without conversion.
3. In every requirement, `courseTopicIds` lists alternative course topics and `assignmentTopicIds` lists alternative **selected** exercises that practise that particular requirement. Every requirement must resolve on both sides. Keep qualifications at their stated level: introductory exercises do not establish advanced expertise. Do not add a role merely because a broad course title sounds related.
4. Set undergraduate eligibility only when no stated restriction excludes undergraduates; leave `eligibilityResolved` false when a required graduation year/institution/GPA etc. cannot be established. Semester bounds support explicit year-of-study requirements; availability, relocation and individual interest are displayed rather than inferred. Never set a fee-based program eligible.
5. Update `AiMlInternshipCatalog.CONTENT_VERSION` on every catalog revision, including a verification-only refresh. The controller combines this with the assignment content version; older browser reports then need resubmission. Update this ledger too.
6. Run backend matching/date tests, the frontend expiry/render checks and the frontend build. Verify included links again before release.

The backend and browser both stop displaying a role at the earlier of its deadline and **verifiedAt + 7 × 24 hours**. Future/malformed verification dates are rejected. Cached reports are filtered again when opened, when the page regains focus/visibility, and at the next expiry while open. No automated browsing or recurring task has been created. The initial verification window ends **3 November 2026 at 18:05:27 IST**, unless a role closes earlier.
